# Workspace Rules - Indonesian Marketing Agent (Better Future Focus)

This file contains general guidelines and constraints for generating marketing copy, campaigns, and automation structures for Better Future (ISCA).

## Target Audience & Channels

1. **Indonesia-First Channels**:
   - **Primary Channels**: Instagram (visual/ Reels), TikTok (short-form video), WhatsApp (transactional & sales closing).
   - **Secondary Channels**: Website landing pages.
   - **Avoid**: Heavy email nurturing or long-form email newsletters as primary lead acquisition tools.

2. **Sales Funnel Direction**:
   - Ad/Content (IG/TikTok) ➔ DM Automation (ManyChat) ➔ WhatsApp Chat with Sales/Counselor ➔ Purchase/Deal.
   - The primary call to action (CTA) for high-intent leads should always lead to a **WhatsApp conversation**.

## Copywriting & Tone Guidelines (Fear & Loss Aversion)

*   **Language**: Conversational, persuasive, and consultative Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia).
*   **The Power of Fear (Loss Aversion)**: Indonesian buyers (both parents and school owners) respond significantly stronger to *preventing losses* than *gaining benefits*.
*   **B2C Parents Hooks (Better Future)**:
    *   Focus on the cost of "Salah Jurusan" (wasting 4 years + Rp 100M+ tuition fees).
*   **B2B School Owners Hooks (Better Future)**:
    *   Focus on losing PPDB students to neighboring schools by failing to offer scientific, modern talent mapping to parents during enrollment.

## Product & Pricing Reference (A/B Testing)

### 1. Better Future (ISCA)
*   **Type**: One-time personality and talent mapping tool.
*   **Value Proposition / Upsell**: Partners with a PPDB consultant to boost school revenue by optimizing enrollment strategies and school entry paths.
*   **Testing Prices**:
    *   *Control Price Set:* Basic: Free | Advanced: Rp 70.000 | AI: Rp 80.000 | Advanced + AI: Rp 120.000
    *   *Variant Price Set:* Basic: Free | Advanced: Rp 99.000 | AI: Rp 99.000 | Advanced + AI: Rp 149.000
