# Product Marketing Context: Better Future (ISCA)

*Last updated: 2026-07-15*

## Product Overview

*   **One-liner:** Premium personality and talent mapping to help students find their ideal college major and help schools optimize enrollment.
*   **What it does:** Maps student potential based on 5 core models: MBTI, RIASEC, DISC, 16PF, and Learning Styles (totaling 19 combined factors) to prevent students from choosing the wrong major.
*   **Product category:** Education Technology (EdTech) / Talent Mapping & Student Development.
*   **Product type:** B2B2C Assessment Tool & AI Counselor.
*   **Business model:** Hybrid (One-time test fees + B2B school dashboard licensing + premium human counselor upsells).

---

## Product Details & Pricing Tiers

### Better Future (ISCA)
*   **Pricing Tiers (A/B Tested):**
    *   **Control Tier (Standard):**
        *   *Basic:* Free (Teaser report)
        *   *Advanced:* Rp 70.000 (Detailed 19-factor report + printable PDF)
        *   *AI:* Rp 80.000 (Consultation with an AI counselor based on test results)
        *   *Advanced + AI:* Rp 120.000 (Bundle)
    *   **Variant Tier (Premium Anchor):**
        *   *Basic:* Free (Teaser report)
        *   *Advanced:* Rp 99.000 (Detailed 19-factor report + printable PDF)
        *   *AI:* Rp 99.000 (Consultation with an AI counselor based on test results)
        *   *Advanced + AI:* Rp 149.000 (Bundle)
*   **Outputs:**
    *   *For Students/Parents:* Comprehensive 19-factor PDF report, career path matching, and terfokus in-app AI guidance.
    *   *For Schools:* Student mapping dashboard, 30, 60, and 180-day activity recommendations to manage student potential, and enrollment growth consulting/advice.
*   **Best Time to Take:** School entry/enrollment (PPDB), school transfer, or annually to monitor development.

---

## Target Audience & Psychology (Fear & Loss Aversion)

**B2C (Parents & Students):**
*   **Primary B2C Pain Points:** Anxiety about their child choosing the wrong study path/major, high costs of traditional offline psychological tests, and lack of clear guidance for college.
*   **Core Fear Hook:** *Salah Jurusan* (Wrong Major). Over 87% of Indonesian students feel they picked the wrong major, leading to wasted college tuition fees (Rp 100M+).
    *   *Example Fear Copy:* *"Sebelum bayar uang kuliah Rp 100 Juta+, pastikan anak Anda tidak 'salah jurusan'. Petakan bakat mereka sekarang dengan Better Future."*

**B2B (Schools/Yayasan):**
*   **Primary B2B Pain Points:** Drop-offs in student enrollment (PPDB) and difficulty showing parents the unique value of the school's educational path.
*   **Core Fear Hook:** Losing prospective students to neighboring schools because the school fails to offer modern, data-driven student development planning to parents during enrollment.

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

1.  **Traditional Offline Psychology Tests**
    *   *Falls short because:* They are expensive (Rp 500.000 - Rp 1.000.000 per student), require booking weeks in advance, take days to generate a paper report, and do not offer continuous/interactive AI counseling.
2.  **Basic Online Personality Tests (Free/Aku Pintar)**
    *   *Falls short because:* They only map a single factor (like MBTI only or RIASEC only) without combining them, lack 19-factor depth, do not provide printable high-quality PDF blueprints, and do not offer customized school activity plans.
